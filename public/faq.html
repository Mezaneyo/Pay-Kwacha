Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about PayKwacha.

Getting Started

How do I create a PayKwacha account?+
Simply click "Dashboard" in the top menu, enter your email and password, and you'll have a business account in seconds. No credit card required, no lengthy approval process.
Do I need a registered business to sign up?+
You can create an account and test with our sandbox right away. To go live and receive real payments, you'll need to provide business registration and KYC documents.
How long does verification take?+
Standard verification is completed within 1-2 business days once we receive all required documents. In many cases it's faster.

Payments & Money

Which mobile money networks do you support?+
We support both Airtel Money and TNM Mpamba through a single unified API, powered by pawaPay's licensed aggregator infrastructure.
What are your transaction fees?+
We charge a flat 2.5% fee on each successful transaction. There are no setup fees, no monthly fees, and no hidden charges.
When do I receive my money?+
Standard settlement is T+1 (next business day) to your registered mobile money or bank account. Instant settlement is available on request for high-volume merchants.
Is there a minimum or maximum amount?+
There is no minimum for accepting payments. Maximum limits are set by the mobile money networks themselves (typically K500,000 per transaction).
What happens if a customer doesn't pay?+
If a customer doesn't complete the payment, the transaction automatically fails after a timeout period. You can choose to retry or cancel the order.

Developers & API

How do I integrate PayKwacha into my app?+
Use our single REST endpoint to initiate payments. We have SDKs for Node.js, Python, and PHP, plus a full sandbox environment to test against. See the API docs for details.
Do you offer webhooks?+
Yes. We send real-time webhooks whenever a payment succeeds, fails, or is pending. This lets your system react instantly without polling.
Is there a sandbox for testing?+
Absolutely. Every account comes with free sandbox access. Test your integration with simulated payments before going live.
What happens if my API key is exposed?+
Contact us immediately at mezaneyo2@gmail.com and we'll rotate your key. Never commit API keys to public repositories.

Security & Compliance

Is PayKwacha secure?+
Yes. All transactions are encrypted in transit and at rest. We operate under pawaPay's Reserve Bank of Malawi licensed infrastructure and follow strict PCI-DSS guidelines.
Do you store customer card or PIN data?+
No. We never see, store, or process card details or PINs. All sensitive authentication is handled directly by the mobile money networks.
Are you compliant with Malawian regulations?+
Yes. PayKwacha operates under pawaPay's Reserve Bank of Malawi licensed infrastructure, which handles all regulatory compliance on our behalf.
