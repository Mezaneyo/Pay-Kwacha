1. Information we collect

When you use PayKwacha, we collect the following types of information:

Information you give us

  • Account information: Email address, password (encrypted), business name, phone number
  • Business details: Business type, settlement mobile money number, description
  • Support communications: Messages you send to our support team

Information we collect automatically

  • Transaction data: Payment amounts, phone numbers, timestamps, status, provider used
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, device type (for security and analytics)
  • Usage data: Pages visited, features used (anonymized)

2. How we use your data

We use your data to:

  • Process payments and settle funds to your account
  • Verify your identity for compliance with Malawian regulations
  • Send transaction receipts and important account notifications
  • Improve our platform and develop new features
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security threats
  • Respond to your support requests

We never sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Sharing your data

We share data only with trusted partners necessary to operate the service:

  • pawaPay: Our licensed payment infrastructure provider (Reserve Bank of Malawi)
  • Airtel Money and TNM Mpamba: To process your mobile money transactions
  • Firebase (Google): For secure authentication and data storage
  • Legal authorities: When required by Malawian law or valid court order

4. Data storage & security

Your data is stored on encrypted servers managed by Firebase (Google Cloud) and pawaPay, both of which maintain industry-leading security certifications. We use:

  • TLS/SSL encryption for all data in transit
  • AES-256 encryption for data at rest
  • Regular security audits and penetration testing
  • Strict access controls and monitoring

Data is retained for as long as your account is active, plus 7 years as required by Malawian financial regulations.

5. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data at any time
  • Correct inaccurate information in your account
  • Delete your account (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Export your transaction history
  • Opt out of marketing communications

To exercise any of these rights, email mezaneyo2@gmail.com.

6. Cookies

We use minimal cookies for:

  • Keeping you logged in (session cookies)
  • Remembering your preferences
  • Anonymous analytics (page views, feature usage)

You can disable cookies in your browser, though some features may stop working.

7. Children's privacy

PayKwacha is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe a minor has created an account, contact us immediately.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced via email and on this page. Continued use of the service constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
