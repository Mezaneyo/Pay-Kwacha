FAQ

Common questions

How quickly can I start accepting payments?+
Sign up takes about 2 minutes. Once your business is verified, you'll get an API key and can start accepting payments immediately — often the same day.
Which mobile money networks do you support?+
We support both Airtel Money and TNM Mpamba through a single unified API, powered by pawaPay's licensed aggregator infrastructure.
When do I receive my money?+
Settlements are made to your registered mobile money or bank account. Standard settlement is T+1 (next business day), with instant settlement available on request.
Is PayKwacha secure?+
Yes. All transactions are encrypted in transit and at rest. We operate under pawaPay's Reserve Bank of Malawi licensed infrastructure and never store card or PIN data.
Do I need a registered business to sign up?+
You can start testing with a sandbox account right away. To go live and receive real payments, you'll need a registered business and KYC documents.
