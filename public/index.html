FAQ

Common questions

How quickly can I start accepting payments?+
Sign up takes about 2 minutes. You'll get your API key and dashboard access immediately.
Which mobile money networks do you support?+
We support both Airtel Money and TNM Mpamba through a single unified API, powered by PayChangu.
When do I receive my money?+
Payments credit your PayKwacha balance immediately. Withdraw anytime to your Airtel Money or TNM Mpamba number.
Is PayKwacha secure?+
Yes. All transactions are encrypted in transit and at rest. We never store card or PIN data.
What's the transaction fee?+
We charge a flat 2% per successful transaction. No setup fees, no monthly fees.
How long are transactions stored?+
Transactions are stored for 3 months in your dashboard, then automatically removed.
