Our story

PayKwacha was born from a simple frustration: accepting digital payments in Malawi shouldn't be this hard. Businesses were juggling multiple providers, dealing with slow settlement times, and struggling with APIs designed for other markets.

We set out to change that. By leveraging pawaPay's unified mobile money infrastructure, we built a platform that works for the Malawian context — supporting both Airtel Money and TNM Mpamba through a single integration, with local support and local expertise.

Today, we're proud to help businesses across Malawi accept payments faster, settle quicker, and grow with confidence.
